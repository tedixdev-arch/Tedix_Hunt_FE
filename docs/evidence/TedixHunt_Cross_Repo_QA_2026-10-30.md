### QA-01 — concurrent refresh clears valid session

**Claim:** concurrent access-token refresh causes session loss; reported reproduced by Claude. **Paths:** F `src/services/api/client.ts` (`send`, `refreshSession`), `src/services/api/session.ts`, `src/services/api/auth.ts` (`refresh`, bootstrap); B `src/routes/auth.ts` (`/refresh`), `src/models/RefreshToken.ts` (`consume`). **Current verification:** confirmed. FE has no shared refresh promise/session generation guard; each 401 can read the same token. BE atomically consumes with `DELETE ... RETURNING`, so reuse fails; FE then unconditionally clears storage. A late success can also overwrite a replacement session; that second race is source-derived, not separately reproduced.

**Reproduction:** independently reproduced locally with the hash-matched current ApiClient and an injected fetch transport. Two authenticated requests receive 401; both POST refresh; the first gets a rotated pair and succeeds; then the second receives 401 invalid_refresh and clears the valid saved pair. Assertions: refresh calls=2, first request succeeds, second rejects, final session=null. This models the inspected single-use BE behavior; no real server/PostgreSQL or production reproduction. The original Claude reproduction remains unavailable.

**Severity/impact:** High reliability; proposed P0 because ordinary concurrent requests can log out authenticated users across roles; not a demonstrated authorization bypass. **Owner:** FE, BE contract/regression support. **Mapping/dependencies:** existing A5/A4 foundations; preserve single-use BE rotation and account revocation. **Bounded correction:** one refresh flight per active session, stale 401 reuse of current valid tokens, no older failure clearing or older success replacing newer login/logout state; coordinate explicit AuthApi.refresh/bootstrap entry points in existing client/store without a new auth layer. **Tests/closure:** concurrent 401 success/failure, one refresh per session, replay rejection/rotation, requests arriving after rotation, network failure, invalid response, logout/login/account switch during refresh and two-client/tab scope decision; verify with real BE+PostgreSQL after source tests. Close on reviewed PR/SHA, passing deterministic regressions and separately recorded integration outcomes; define cross-tab scope explicitly, do not claim it solved by a per-instance promise alone.

**Severity/impact:** provisional High / P1 for unauthenticated DB growth and auth resource abuse; final exploitability/severity needs operator evidence of ingress controls, traffic and capacity. **Owner:** BE with deployment operator/PO policy. **Mapping/dependencies:** A4/A6/B4 security and L1 deletion context; existing foundation rows do not automatically authorize new limiter/retention scope. **Bounded correction:** reuse middleware for auth and guest-creation limits, deliberate proxy-hop/IP trust and retry guidance; design guest retention/cleanup protecting active Hunts, membership/history/audit and privacy. No IP-only threshold that blocks a supervised class behind NAT; do not trust arbitrary X-Forwarded-For. **Tests/closure:** bounded disposable-environment bursts, 429/Retry-After and recovery, independent identities, shared NAT, forged forwarding headers/proxy chain, login/refresh compatibility, cleanup dry-run and active-Hunt/reference protection. Require current ingress/proxy config, approved thresholds/retention rules, implementation evidence and measured acceptance; do not conclude “no production protection” from repository absence.

### QA-06 — credentialless registration semantics

**Claim:** participant registration accepts email without password. **Paths:** B `src/routes/auth.ts` (`/participant/register` and OpenAPI), `src/models/User.ts`; F `src/pages/ParticipantJoinFlow.tsx`, `src/services/api/auth.ts`. **Verification/disposition:** partially verified: BE explicitly supports credentialless registration; email+password takes credential path, otherwise creates participant with name only. Email-only input is discarded; `isGuest` defaults false, distinct from `/guest`, and tokens include refresh. FE registration requires password and its typed RegisterInput requires all fields. Thus backend behavior is real, but not demonstrated as an accidental FE password omission or an approved guest-equivalence design.

**Severity/impact:** Medium provisional / P1–P2 policy and recovery ambiguity; users of other callers could expect a recoverable email account that was never stored. Not evidence of password bypass into an existing account. **Owner:** BE and PO account policy, FE contract alignment. **Mapping/dependencies:** A4/A6/H3; preserve disabled/unverified Tedix-ID linking and professional approval rules. **Bounded correction:** decide explicit native versus credentialless semantics, then validate email/password combinations and align response/UI/OpenAPI. Do not silently require credentials for all Participants or promote guest rights. **Tests/closure:** absent/email-only/password-only/both, malformed types and weak password, duplicate/native recovery, `isGuest`/refresh/privilege behavior and rejected external-ID link; approved decision plus persisted API tests and integration proof required. Until policy is settled, disposition remains partial, not accepted design or resolved defect.

### QA-08 — guest expiry versus continuation

**Claim:** guest expiry may interrupt long Hunts. **Paths:** B `src/routes/auth.ts` (`/guest`, `/refresh`), `src/lib/jwt.ts`, `src/middleware/auth.ts`; F `src/services/api/auth.ts`, `src/services/api/client.ts`, `src/app/providers/AuthProvider.tsx`; existing H8-EXEC-1. **Verification/disposition:** partially verified. Guest access is explicitly 1h with no refresh; BE rejects expired bearer and guest refresh; FE stores access only and clears after unauthorized recovery fails. Creating another guest creates a new identity, not proof of restoring membership. This establishes expiry and recovery risk, not an implemented gameplay failure after one hour. No long-running real Hunt/session recovery reproduction performed.

**Severity/impact:** Medium provisional / P1–P2: protected requests after expiry may require recovery; actual Hunt length, permitted guest lifecycle and persisted membership determine impact. **Owner:** FE/BE session owners with PO recovery policy. **Mapping/dependencies:** A5/A6 and H8-EXEC-1 after enrollment/start/membership revisions; do not invent ungated runtime. **Bounded correction:** explicit expiry warning/recovery semantics in existing flow after policy decision; preserve identity/membership and revocation. Do not just lengthen tokens or silently make guests refreshable. **Tests/closure:** simulated clock before/at/after 1h, active Hunt/reload/reconnect, same-identity restoration and denied revoked users; real PostgreSQL membership plus approved continuation contract and relevant deployed device proof. Keep gameplay interruption unresolved until reproduced against implemented runtime.
